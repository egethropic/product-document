# Product document

Create a complete visual concept and rebuild the selected design as an editable document using accurate product imagery. A first end-to-end pilot exercised concept selection, background and illustration extraction, CAD replacement and editable export; the user accepted the finished design. Other document formats and products still need their own validation.

1. Establish audience, purpose, format, authoritative copy, product revision, and intended output. Propose copy where useful, but do not invent specifications, claims, prices, contact details, or certifications. Verify technical claims against the relevant revision and primary evidence. Establish folds, panel order, and print requirements where applicable.
2. Use Terra to inspect the actual CAD and render multiple useful views before concept generation. Capture recognizable sides and details appropriate to the product. When realistic product imagery is requested, prepare physically plausible materials, restrained surface texture and deliberate studio lighting before sending views to imagegen. Inspect the rendered images for readable recesses, believable metal reflections and suitable shadows; changing roughness or increasing samples alone does not establish realism. Record CAD revision, render provenance, camera, framing, material and lighting decisions, and model limitations. Do not present a render as evidence of qualified performance.
3. Give imagegen the actual rendered views, authoritative document content, and image and text design references. Generate complete, flat document concepts including typography, product placement, background, and decoration. Generated lettering and hardware are visual references, not authoritative assets. Present alternatives for selection; a request to finish a specified concept is selection. Only selected concepts proceed to production.
4. Edit the selected concept with imagegen to retain only its background. Then use separate imagegen edits of the selected original to extract custom decorative illustrations with transparent backgrounds. Inspect both for remnants and artifacts. Keep complex paintings and textures raster by default.
5. Replace **every** generated product depiction, including illustrated ones, with a fresh render from the actual CAD. Match the selected depiction's orientation, camera, framing, appropriate real materials, texture, and lighting without deforming geometry. Label speculative finishes. Preserve source proportions and verify silhouette, connectors, openings, controls, and markings. If a concept depicts an impossible view, adjust the composition. Never use a product cropped from the generated concept as final artwork.
6. Rebuild authoritative text, layout, and simple geometry as editable vectors while preserving the selected concept's appearance. Use separate layers for background, decorative illustrations, product renders, and live text. SVG is a useful default; use the requested editable format when appropriate. Vectorize suitable illustrations or background when requested and appropriate, checking fidelity. An embedded raster is still raster. Use authoritative logos and licensed fonts; retain live text and provide an outlined export separately if needed.
7. Render and inspect every page at normal and enlarged sizes. Compare the final composition with the concept, copy with its authority, and each product with CAD. In technical copy preserve signs, decimals, ranges, units and case-sensitive pin identifiers; do not normalize all punctuation away. Check glyphs, wrapping, clipping, contrast, panel order, bleed and output requirements. Verify the exported PDF's actual page count and physical page size; an SVG preview alone does not catch pagination or print-margin errors. Deliver a hybrid editable SVG and PDF by default, or the requested formats, plus a preview, assets and provenance. Identify raster elements and unresolved limitations; product renders and the default background remain raster.

## Execution model

Terra is responsible for CAD, content, composition, and verification; imagegen creates concepts and edits. Terra is the preferred executor; the baseline is `gpt-6.1-terra` when the host confirms it is supported. An explicitly requested model or model-comparison trial overrides this preference: run the same workflow with that model and record the override. Resolve the exact model from the host catalog at run start, respect an explicit version pin, and record catalog source, exact model ID, reasoning effort, and service tier when exposed. Record unavailable fields as unavailable; do not invent them or claim the baseline is the latest. If the required Terra model is unavailable, report the mismatch and obtain a substitution decision. Do not automatically hand work to Sol or silently change model families.
